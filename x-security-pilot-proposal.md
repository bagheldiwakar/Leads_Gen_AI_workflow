# X-Security Pilot Proposal

## Purpose
Validate whether X-Security can solve a specific endpoint-security problem for one qualified partner or direct customer.

## Recommended pilot scope
- 25–100 endpoints
- 30 days
- One defined user group, client environment or business unit
- One executive sponsor and one technical owner
- Weekly review with documented findings

## Pilot objectives
- Confirm endpoint visibility and policy coverage
- Test the agreed prevention and response workflows
- Measure deployment time and operational effort
- Evaluate reporting usefulness for the technical team and leadership
- Decide whether a broader rollout is justified

## Required entry criteria
- A named business problem
- A current endpoint-security baseline
- An executive sponsor
- A technical owner
- Written success measures
- Permission to run the agreed test

## Success measures
- Percentage of intended endpoints enrolled
- Time needed to deploy and configure
- Relevant prevention or detection events observed
- Time to investigate/respond in the agreed workflow
- Operator feedback on usability and reporting
- Decision: expand, revise or stop

## Pilot close-out
Deliver a short results summary, compare baseline with outcomes, agree next steps and request permission to use a non-confidential case study if the result is positive.
