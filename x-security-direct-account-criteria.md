# Direct-Account Targeting Criteria

## Ideal direct customer
Prioritize organizations that have:
- A meaningful managed endpoint estate
- Distributed or remote users
- Compliance or security-reporting pressure
- A current security transformation, tool consolidation or endpoint-management initiative
- A clear technology and security leadership structure

## Target roles
- CTO or CIO: architecture and IT operating model
- CISO or security leader: risk, controls and security outcomes
- Endpoint, workplace or infrastructure leader: technical validation
- Procurement: commercial process after technical qualification

## Do not prioritize yet
- Organizations with no clear endpoint-security use case
- Accounts that cannot support a pilot
- Accounts where no executive sponsor is accessible
- Targets selected solely because they are large or famous
