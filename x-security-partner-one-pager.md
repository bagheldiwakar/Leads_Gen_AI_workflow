# X-Security Partner Opportunity

## Who this is for
MSSPs, systems integrators, managed-service providers, security resellers and telecommunications security teams.

## Partner opportunity
X-Security is intended to become a partner-ready endpoint-security offer that providers can recommend, resell or manage for their own customers.

## Partner value
- Add an endpoint-security option to the existing portfolio
- Create recurring managed-service revenue
- Improve centralized endpoint operations and reporting
- Offer a limited pilot before committing to a broad rollout

## Initial engagement
1. Discovery call to understand the partner's customer base and endpoint-security model
2. Review the proposed pilot scope
3. Choose referral, resale or managed-service motion
4. Run a controlled pilot
5. Decide on wider commercial packaging based on evidence

## Ask
A 15-minute discussion to determine whether an endpoint-security pilot fits the partner's managed-security strategy.
