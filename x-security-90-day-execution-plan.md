# X-Security 90-Day Go-to-Market Execution Plan

## Goal
Validate that X-Security can earn qualified buyer meetings and pilot commitments before attempting broad-scale sales.

## Operating model
- 70% partner route: MSSPs, systems integrators, managed-service providers and security resellers.
- 30% direct route: companies with large endpoint estates, security/compliance pressure or distributed workforces.
- Sell outcomes: visibility, prevention, response speed, manageable operations and customer reporting.

## Days 1–14: Prepare a credible offer
1. Finalize the narrow pilot described in `x-security-pilot-proposal.md`.
2. Use the product story, demo script, comparison checklist and partner one-pager in every conversation.
3. Select the first 10 partner targets from the master tracker.
4. Verify a current primary and secondary decision-maker for each target.
5. Personalize the corresponding target brief before outreach.

## Days 15–30: Start discovery
1. Send a personalized LinkedIn connection request or verified business email.
2. Ask for a 15-minute discovery call; do not pitch a full replacement in the first message.
3. Use the discovery questions in each target brief.
4. Update the master tracker after every action.
5. Review weekly: messages sent, replies, meetings and qualified opportunities.

## Days 31–60: Qualify and pilot
1. Confirm a real endpoint-security problem, executive sponsor, deployment owner and success criteria.
2. Offer a pilot only when all four are present.
3. For partners, choose a referral, resale or managed-service pilot model.
4. For direct accounts, propose a controlled proof of value.
5. Record baseline metrics before the pilot starts.

## Days 61–90: Convert and improve
1. Measure coverage, prevention/detection results, response effort, deployment effort and reporting quality.
2. Turn a successful pilot into a case study and repeatable sales story.
3. Increase effort on target segments and messages that produce qualified meetings.
4. Pause low-fit segments and revisit only when a credible new trigger appears.

## Weekly scorecard
- Contacts verified
- Personalized messages sent
- Positive replies
- Discovery calls booked
- Qualified opportunities
- Pilots proposed
- Pilots active
- Pilots converted
