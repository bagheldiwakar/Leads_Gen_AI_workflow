# Endpoint-Security Evaluation Checklist

Use this checklist to compare X-Security with the buyer's current approach.

## Coverage
- Can all intended endpoint types be enrolled?
- Is coverage visible centrally?
- Can policies be assigned consistently?

## Prevention and detection
- Does the solution support the required prevention controls?
- Can the team investigate relevant activity?
- Can the team respond through an agreed workflow?

## Operations
- Is deployment practical at the buyer's scale?
- Are policy changes auditable?
- Can managed-service teams operate across multiple client environments?

## Reporting
- Are operational reports clear?
- Are executive-level outcomes understandable?
- Can the provider show value to end customers?

## Commercial and risk
- Is the scope and support model clear?
- Are integrations and migration dependencies understood?
- What must be proven in a pilot before procurement?
